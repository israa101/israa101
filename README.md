## 👋 About Me

**I'm Israa Hamdy Hussein** — a 3rd-year Data Science & AI student at **Alexandria National University** (Class of 2027), based in **Alexandria, Egypt 🇪🇬**.

I got hooked on the idea that a few thousand matrix multiplications can recognize a face, catch a lie, or stop a cyberattack — so I've spent my degree proving it, one project at a time.

**What I obsess over:**

- 👁️ **Computer Vision** — teaching cameras to actually understand what they see
- 🗣️ **NLP** — especially Arabic, because it deserves better tooling
- 🛡️ **Cybersecurity AI** — models that defend, not just predict
- 🎬 **Recommender Systems** — matching people with what they'll love
- 🌐 **Edge AI & Federated Learning** — intelligence without the cloud bill

**My superpower:** turning a messy dataset into a working pipeline before the coffee gets cold ☕
**Right now:** stacking certifications, competitions, and side projects into a portfolio that speaks before I do.

> *"Let's build something that actually works."* 🚀
